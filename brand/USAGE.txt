USUCGER LOGO PACK
=================

Files
  logo/*.svg        Vector masters. Text is outlined; no fonts needed. Use these
                    wherever possible (Illustrator, InDesign, Inkscape, web,
                    PowerPoint 2016+, Word 2016+, Google Slides).
  png/*.png         3000 px wide transparent PNGs for older Office versions and
                    email signatures.
  USUCGER-logo-sheet.pdf   One vector PDF of every variant, for printers.

Which file to use
  usucger-logo-horizontal            default on white/light backgrounds
  usucger-logo-horizontal-reversed   on cobalt (#102747) or dark backgrounds
  usucger-logo-horizontal-fullname   letterhead, certificates, formal documents
  usucger-logo-stacked               square spaces, posters, slide title pages
  usucger-logo-*-mono-black/white    single-ink print, engraving, embroidery, fax
  usucger-mark                       favicons, avatars, stamps, or beside the name
  usucger-icon                       app icon / social profile picture

Colours (theme: Strata - cobalt & amber)
  Cobalt Deep  #102747   Cobalt #1B3F6E   Cobalt Bright #2554A0
  Amber        #C47A1A   Amber Highlight #FCD87A
  Typefaces: Fraunces (wordmark, headings), Figtree (tagline, body) - Google Fonts.

Rules
  Keep clear space equal to the height of the wordmark's U on all sides.
  Minimum width: horizontal lockup 32 mm / 120 px; mark alone 8 mm / 30 px.
  Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, add effects, or re-set the wordmark in
  another typeface.
