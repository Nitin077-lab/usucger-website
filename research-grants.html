Small Research Grants

USUCGER seed funding for projects that improve how geotechnical engineering is taught. Every funded project delivers free, reusable material for the whole community.

Funded Projects

What the grants have produced

Every funded project delivers something the whole community can use — activity libraries, physical demonstrations, video series and open-source software. All of it is free.

🏗
Geotechnical Education Modelers (GEMs)
2020–2021   Dr. Fawad Niazi · Purdue University Fort Wayne · $5,000 from USUCGER
A two-year project developing physical educational models for soil–structure interaction, co-funded by CELT ($2,500) and the CME Department ($4,000). Two video walkthroughs document the models and how to use them in class.
Video part 1 → Video part 2 →
✨
Soil Magic 2.0
2017–2018   Dr. Hoyoung Seo · Texas Tech University
Eight classroom demonstration activities designed to produce “aha” moments in soil mechanics, each paired with a video demonstration and a learning module.
Project report (PDF) →
🤝
Active Learning Activities for Undergraduate Soil Mechanics
2016–2017   Dr. Kristin Sample-Lord (Villanova) & Dr. Gretchen Bohnhoff (UW–Platteville)
Sixteen group activities in editable formats, each with an instructor summary sheet, learning objectives, handout, solution set, example rubric and supplemental information. Activities run from one to fifty minutes.
Download the library → Give feedback → See on teaching aids →
💻
geotech — Geotechnical Engineering Software Package in R
2014–2015   Dr. James Kaklamanos · Merrimack College
A free R package covering phase diagrams and index parameters, grain-size distributions, plasticity, classification, compaction, groundwater, subsurface stresses, Mohr circles, consolidation, shear strength, bearing capacity, lateral earth pressures, slope stability and subsurface exploration.
Package on CRAN → Software page → Final report (PDF) → Presentation (PDF) →

Calls for small research grant proposals are announced to the membership by email and at the annual business meeting. To be notified, make sure your membership is current and you are on the USUCGER list. Questions: uboard@usucger.org.
