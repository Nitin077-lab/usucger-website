Membership & Dues

Join the USUCGER Community

Participation of all people with a vested interest in geotechnical research and teaching is encouraged. Current cycle: January 2025 – December 2027 at $75 per person.

Category A — U.S. Universities
$75 per person · 3-year cycle
Eligibility. Faculty — including professors, lecturers, instructors and researchers, active or emeritus, full- or part-time. Graduate students may participate when sponsored by the contact person. Each institution must have a paid member who serves as the contact person.

How membership works

Payment is made in three-year increments. Whichever category you fall into, the dues are the same — $75 per person for the full cycle — and they are not prorated, so joining partway through a cycle costs the same as joining at the start.

Choosing your category

Category A (U.S. Universities) is for people affiliated with a U.S. academic institution that has a geotechnical research or education program. Each member institution designates a contact person, who must be a paid member; that contact person is also the institution's delegate for Council voting, and may sponsor graduate students.

Category B (Associates) is for everyone else with a stake in the field — faculty at non-U.S. institutions, consultants and practitioners, agency staff, and retired members of the community. Associate membership is a single seat and is non-voting.

What happens after you pay

  • You are added to the USUCGER email list, which reaches roughly 300 members in the U.S. and abroad.
  • Your name and affiliation appear in the public membership directory.
  • Your institution appears on the member universities list with you as the contact, if you are the designated contact person.
  • Your students become eligible to apply for travel grants, and you become eligible to nominate colleagues for awards.

Institutional membership rules

Under the By-Laws, academic institutions in the United States with a program of research and/or education in geotechnical engineering are eligible for Council membership, subject to approval of the Board of Directors. Each member institution designates a delegate with voting rights, and holds two votes — one to be cast for a junior faculty candidate and one for a senior faculty candidate in Board elections. An institution ceases to be a voting member on failure to pay dues by the end of the membership year.

Questions

Membership, dues and directory questions go to the Membership Coordinator or the webmaster at webmaster@usucger.org. See the contact page for the full list of addresses.
