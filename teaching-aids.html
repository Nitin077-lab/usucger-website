Resources & Media

Teaching Aids

Free, classroom-ready material contributed by the USUCGER community and allied organizations — activities, modules, course notes, exam banks and lab demonstrations.

🧩
Library of Group Activities Promoting Active Learning in Undergraduate Soil Mechanics
Sixteen different activities developed with the help of undergraduate students. Each activity includes an instructor summary sheet with learning objectives and instructions, the activity handout, the solution set, an example rubric and supplemental information. Activities range from one or two minutes up to fifty minutes.
Download the library → Funded by a USUCGER grant →
🧠
Geotech CAT-Apps for Building Critical Thinking Skills
Thirteen CAT-App questions, each with a problem statement and three to four prompts. Anticipated student responses are supplied for every question in three categories — strong, average and weak — so you can calibrate discussion and grading.
Open the question set →
✨
Soil Magic 2.0 — classroom demonstrations
Eight classroom demonstration activities with accompanying videos and learning modules, developed at Texas Tech University to create “aha” moments in soil mechanics.
Report & activity table (PDF) → Project details →
🏗
Geotechnical Education Modelers (GEMs)
Physical models for teaching soil–structure interaction, developed at Purdue University Fort Wayne with USUCGER support. Two videos document construction and classroom use.
Part 1 → Part 2 →
📚
USUCGER 2020 Geotechnical Engineering Faculty Teaching Strategies & Resources Workshop
Workshop report and materials aimed at helping geotechnical faculty at all career stages become more effective and efficient teachers, plus a bank of exam questions and solutions contributed by participants for use in undergraduate geo-engineering courses.
Report & materials (PDF) → Exam problems (PDF) →
🌍
Introductory Course on Geoenvironmental Engineering
Resources to prepare you to teach an introductory geoenvironmental engineering course: challenges in teaching the subject, relevant course content, a course syllabus, teaching resources (textbook, homework questions, projects and exams) and example lecture slides.
Course resources → Contact: kreddy@uic.edu
🌴
Course Notes: Dynamic Response of Soil and Soil-Foundation Systems
Full lecture notes for RPI CIVL 6540, addressed mostly to geotechnical and structural master's and PhD students.
Open the notes → Contact: zeghal@rpi.edu
♻
Introductory Course on Sustainable Engineering
A short course addressing the growing urgency of incorporating sustainability into engineering curricula. It covers current challenges, presents the content of a new textbook, and offers guidance on selecting topics and preparing a syllabus.
Course resources → Contact: kreddy@uic.edu
🎥
Recorded lecture courses
A complete 26-lecture introductory geotechnical engineering course, chapter-by-chapter webcasts aligned to Coduto, Yeung & Kitch, and a full soil mechanics laboratory on video.
Go to videos & webinars →
🦠
Biogeotechnics Education & Outreach Materials
The NSF-funded Center for Bio-mediated and Bio-inspired Geotechnics (CBBG) has developed a series of biogeotechnics modules for K–12 and university use, including a PowerPoint lesson for introductory civil engineering courses and 36 lesson plans developed by K–12 teachers.
Intro module (PPTX) → K–12 lesson plans →
🗺
Engineering Geology and Site Characterization Educational Resources
Six modules covering engineering geology, rock properties and engineering characterization, site characterization, geologic mapping and geologic hazards — plus an SPT video and PowerPoint slides.
Open the modules →
🌱
Introduction to Geosynthetics
Four short video presentations making up a one-hour introductory lecture, giving students an overview of geosynthetics, their types and their functions. Delivered by Professor Jorge G. Zornberg, University of Texas at Austin, with a companion site for educators.
For students → For educators →
🎓
Science Education Resource Center (Carleton College)
An extensive collection of online teaching resources and peer-reviewed activities that can be incorporated into geotechnical engineering, geology, geohazards and earthquake engineering courses.
Visit SERC →
🌐
Geoengineer.org Education Section
Texts, notes, slides, videos and photos grouped under a wide variety of geotechnical topics. You can search for specific topics and contribute your own content.
Visit Geoengineer.org →
📊
Case Western element test database
Over 234 tests on three different sands in dense and loose conditions, plus 18 tests on Edgar Plastic Kaolin clay and 8 on Hydrite 121 clay — useful for validating constitutive relations or setting student projects.
Open the database →
💻
Software & virtual laboratories
The 1,700-program GGSD directory, the open-source geotech R package and a virtual simulator for advanced geotechnical laboratory testing.
Go to software & data →
✉ Contribute
These pages are built entirely from what the community shares. If you have activities, notes, modules or videos that other geotechnical instructors could use, send a short description and a link to webmaster@usucger.org.

USUCGERGeotechnical Education & Research

United States Universities Council on Geotechnical Education and Research. Founded 1985 in San Francisco — advocacy for geomechanical, geotechnical and geoenvironmental engineering research and education at U.S. universities.
