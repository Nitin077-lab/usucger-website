Resources & Media

Videos & Webinars

Complete recorded courses, international webinar series and a full soil mechanics laboratory on video — all free.

Recorded Courses

Full lecture courses

🎥
Introduction to Geotechnical Engineering — Dr. Jean-Louis Briaud
A full set of 26 fifty-minute recorded lectures from Dr. Briaud's introductory geotechnical engineering course. The first twelve lectures each open with a 10–15 minute case history to draw students in.
Watch on the USUCGER channel →
📺
Introduction to Geotechnical Engineering webcasts
Webcasts for an introductory geotechnical course, designed to align with Geotechnical Engineering: Principles and Practices, 3rd edition (Coduto, Yeung & Kitch). Playlists are organized chapter by chapter.
Open the playlists →
🎬
uSeeGeo — Geotechnical Engineering Lectures
A further channel of free recorded geotechnical engineering lectures covering core topics for undergraduate and graduate students.
Watch on YouTube →
Webinar Series

Free webinar series

🌍
International Geoenvironmental Webinar Series
Free recorded geoenvironmental engineering webinars from professors and practitioners around the world, targeted at MS and PhD students considering geoenvironmental research. Contact: kreddy@uic.edu
Watch the series →
🌱
International Geosynthetics Society — North America
Free recorded webinars from professors and practitioners on engineering with geosynthetics, aimed at professionals and graduate students.
Watch the series →
🎓
IGS University Lecture Series
Videos and slides from leading professors in the International Geosynthetics Society university lecture programme, for students and professionals alike.

Recorded soil mechanics lab tests

2

3

4

10

United States Universities Council on Geotechnical Education and Research. Founded 1985 in San Francisco — advocacy for geomechanical, geotechnical and geoenvironmental engineering research and education at U.S. universities.
