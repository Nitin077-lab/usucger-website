Student Travel Grants

Up to $1,000 per student toward airfare, hotel, per diem and conference registration for students presenting accepted research, advised by a USUCGER member. Two upcoming application cycles.

Download application info →

The award

The USUCGER Board sponsors students — graduate or undergraduate — to attend conferences in support of their accepted research. Awards are up to $1,000 per student and may be used for airfare, hotel, per diem and/or conference student registration fees.

The Board will evaluate applications during two upcoming cycles and anticipates granting at least one travel award per cycle, depending on available funding. Separate solicitations are emailed by the USUCGER Board for each cycle. The award supports travel to geotechnical, geoenvironmental-focused or interdisciplinary conferences.

Deadlines

Conference datesApplication deadlineAnticipated notification
Jan 1 – Apr 30, 2027 (this cycle)November 15, 2026December 15, 2026
May 1 – Aug 31, 2027 (next cycle)March 15, 2027April 15, 2027

Apply to the cycle that covers your conference dates, not the cycle you happen to be in when you apply.

How to apply

  1. Download the current call (January 1 – April 30, 2027 conferences) for the full instructions.
  2. Confirm your advisor's USUCGER membership is current for the 2025–2027 cycle — if not, they can renew here. Each member may nominate one student per cycle.
  3. Prepare a single PDF named LastName.pdf with your advisor's signed letter, a CV (up to three pages), a one-page essay, an itemized travel budget and the submitted abstract, plus a recorded video of up to three minutes summarizing your research.
  4. Submit the package through the online application form by 11:59 PM Pacific on the deadline for your cycle. Advisors may send their letter separately through this form.

Questions? Email the Board at uboard@usucger.org.

✓ Eligibility requirements
  • ✓Graduate or undergraduate student at a U.S. member university
  • ✓Advised by a current USUCGER member
  • ✓Presenting submitted research (oral or poster) at the conference
  • ✓Research in geotechnical or geoenvironmental engineering
  • ✓Conference focus: geotechnical, geoenvironmental or interdisciplinary
⚠ Advisor membership
Applicants must be advised by a USUCGER member whose membership is current. Check the membership directory before you apply — if your advisor is not listed for the 2025–2027 cycle, they need to renew.

Be part of the geotechnical academic community

Membership is $75 per person for the 2025–2027 cycle and open to faculty, researchers, students and practitioners with a stake in geotechnical education and research.
