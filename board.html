Board of Directors

The Board is comprised of elected members drawn from the geotechnical faculty pool across the nation — four senior and four junior directors serving staggered four-year terms, plus ex-officio members.

✉ Reach the Board
The whole Board can be reached at uboard@usucger.org — use this address for award nominations, travel grant questions and Council business. Website corrections go to webmaster@usucger.org.
🗳 How the Board is elected

Two directors are elected each year — one junior and one senior — by the delegates of the member institutions, and new directors take office at the annual meeting.

Each member institution holds two votes — one for a junior faculty candidate and one for a senior faculty candidate. No proxy votes are allowed.
