What to send where

Post a faculty position

USUCGER member institutions can post academic openings at no charge. Email the webmaster with the position title, institution and a link to the official posting. Listings are removed after one year, so let us know if a search closes early.

Membership and dues

Purchases are handled online through the membership store. If a payment did not go through, your name is missing from the directory, or your institution's contact person has changed, email the webmaster.

Membership & dues →

Not receiving USUCGER emails

The USUCGER list reaches roughly 300 members nationally and internationally. If announcements have stopped arriving, check your spam or quarantine folder first — university filters are the most common cause. If that does not resolve it, email the webmaster and we will verify the address we hold for you.

Award nominations

Nominations for all award categories go to the Board at uboard@usucger.org.

Student travel grants

Applications and questions about eligibility go to the Board. Students must be advised by a current USUCGER member and be presenting accepted research.

Share a conference or resource

The conference list carries events shared by USUCGER members, and the teaching aids and software pages are built entirely from community contributions. If you have something the community should see, send it to the webmaster with a short description and a link.
