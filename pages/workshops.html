Workshops and what they produced

USUCGER workshops are built around a deliverable the whole community can keep using afterwards. Everything below is free.

🎓
USUCGER Geotechnical Engineering Faculty Teaching Strategies & Resources Workshop
2020
A workshop aimed at helping geotechnical faculty at all career stages become more effective and efficient teachers. It produced a full report and materials package, plus a bank of exam questions and solutions contributed by participants for use in undergraduate geo-engineering courses.
Report & materials (PDF) → Exam problems (PDF) →
🌍
Teaching an Introductory Course on Geoenvironmental Engineering
A short course preparing attendees to teach an introductory geoenvironmental engineering course — challenges in teaching the subject, relevant content, a syllabus, teaching resources and example lecture slides.
Course materials → Contact: kreddy@uic.edu
♻
Teaching an Introductory Course on Sustainable Engineering
A short course on incorporating sustainability into engineering curricula: current challenges, the content of a new textbook, and guidance on selecting topics and preparing a syllabus.
Course materials → Contact: kreddy@uic.edu
