The award

The USUCGER Board sponsors students — graduate or undergraduate — to attend conferences in support of their accepted research. Awards are up to $1,000 per student and may be used for airfare, hotel, per diem and/or conference student registration fees.

Applications are evaluated during three annual cycles. At least one award per cycle is anticipated, subject to available funding. The award supports travel to geotechnical, geoenvironmental-focused or interdisciplinary conferences.

How to apply

  1. Download the current application document for the full instructions and the information the Board asks for.
  2. Confirm your advisor's USUCGER membership is current for the 2025–2027 cycle — if not, they can renew here.
  3. Submit your application to the Board at uboard@usucger.org before the deadline for your cycle.
