The Awards

Four awards, one community

Honoring outstanding contributions to geotechnical engineering — from early career breakthroughs to lifetime service and sustained commitment to inclusion.

Every 2 years

Early Career Researcher Award

For tenure-track or tenured faculty affiliates of USUCGER-member institutions who have made significant and innovative contributions to research in geotechnical engineering. Nominees may not exceed 45 years of age or 12 years from the PhD, whichever is more restrictive.

SD
Sheng Dai 2025
5 recipients since 2021
Every 2 years

Early Career Educator Award

For tenure-track or tenured faculty affiliates who have provided exceptional educational and professional development services to the USUCGER community. The same age and post-PhD limits apply as for the researcher award.

4 recipients since 2022
Every 2 years

Distinguished Service Award

For affiliates of USUCGER-member institutions who have given distinguished service to USUCGER. Eligible nominees can be at any rank or position — there is no career-stage restriction.

Diversity, Equity & Inclusion Award

For champions who demonstrate strong and sustained commitment to promoting diversity, equity and inclusion in geotechnical engineering. Eligible nominees can be at any rank or position.

⏰ Nominations
Nominations close January 15, 2026. Send to uboard@usucger.org.
📋 Who can be nominated

Early career awards: tenure-track or tenured faculty at a member institution, no more than 45 years of age and 12 years from the PhD.

Service and DEI awards: any affiliate of a member institution, at any rank or position.
