History & Mission

The foundation of geotechnical knowledge in America

USUCGER was born during the 1985 San Francisco International Conference on Soil Mechanics and Foundation Engineering, to preserve an important source of research funding for the geomechanical, geotechnical and geoenvironmental community — NSF's program in the Directorate of Engineering. The first national meeting followed in Houston, Texas in 1986.

The 1991 Arlington meeting set the long-range course and built lasting coalitions with ASFE, ASCE-GED and ADSC. Today USUCGER is the primary collective voice for geotechnical engineering education and research in U.S. universities.

It is USUCGER's mission to provide advocacy for the continued development and expansion of high quality geomechanical, geotechnical and geoenvironmental engineering research and education which will enhance the welfare of humankind and meet the needs of the nation.
Long-Term Objectives
1
Become a strong and effective advocate for funding of innovative geomechanical, geotechnical and geoenvironmental research and development through proper representation at federal, state and industrial/private agencies.
2
Be a forum for members in gathering and disseminating information on funding opportunities and the results of completed research and development projects.
3
Foster cooperation with other organizations with common goals in identifying R&D needs and providing unified representation in expanding the funding base.
4
Effect long-term positive change in the funding of civil engineering research and development through legislation, by involving the construction industry directly in the funding process.

Membership & Dues

Two ways to belong

Participation of all people with a vested interest in geotechnical research and teaching is encouraged. Current cycle: January 2025 – December 2027.

Category A — U.S. Universities
$75 per person · 3-year cycle
Eligibility: faculty including professors, lecturers, instructors and researchers — active or emeritus, full- or part-time — plus graduate students sponsored by the contact person. Each institution must have a paid member serving as contact person.
  • Voting rights through your institution's delegate
  • Access to the USUCGER email list (~500 members)
  • Free faculty position postings
  • Student travel grant eligibility for your advisees
  • Award nomination eligibility
Purchase University Membership →
Category B — Associates
$75 per person · 3-year cycle
Eligibility: any individual with a vested interest in geotechnical education and research — including faculty at non-U.S. institutions, practitioners, government employees and others. Single seat only.
  • Access to the USUCGER email list
  • Community participation and networking
  • Funding opportunity and announcement alerts
  • Conference and workshop information
